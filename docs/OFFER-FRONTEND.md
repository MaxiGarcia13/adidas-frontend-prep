# Role context (frontend focus)

Condensed from the **Senior Software Engineer (Digital Tech & Brand Technologies)** offer — backend / frontend / fullstack profiles. Use this for interview-coach framing; practice exercises stay frontend-first.

## Purpose

Build digital platforms powering adidas business, products, and consumer experiences. Senior engineers design, build, and operate scalable cloud-native solutions used by millions.

Possible landing areas:

- **Digital Tech** — digital commerce, consumer engagement, customer communications, business platforms
- **Brand & Creation Technologies (BCT)** — design, creation, planning, and delivery of products/experiences

Expect work across modern services and **internal + consumer-facing frontend apps**, APIs, event-driven architectures, and cloud-native platforms, with product, architecture, QA, and platform partners.

## Accountabilities that matter for frontend interviews

- Design and develop applications in a microservices / platform context
- Code quality, **code reviews**, engineering best practices
- Identify and manage technical debt
- Mentor and guide engineers
- Stakeholder communication (PO, QA, architects, SRE, business)
- CI/CD and operating what you ship (ownership mindset)

## What they look for (general)

- ~7+ years IT / product development lifecycle exposure
- Leading engineers or technical initiatives is a plus
- Strong communication; fluent English
- Quality assurance mindset and engineering excellence

## Required — frontend strength

- Strong **TypeScript**, **JavaScript**, modern ECMAScript
- Strong **React** including state management: **Redux**, **MobX**, or **Context API**
- Additional experience with **Angular** or **Vue** is valued
- **HTML5**, **CSS3**, hands-on **SASS/LESS**

## Shared foundations (still relevant in a FE interview)

- **AI-assisted development** (Claude, Copilot, Cursor, etc.): prompting, generation, review, debugging — encouraged in the live exercise
- Microservices / REST APIs; sync and async communication awareness
- Cloud (preferably AWS), containers / Kubernetes familiarity
- Distributed systems: scalability, resilience, **performance**
- Event-driven / messaging awareness
- CI/CD and production troubleshooting mindset
- Testing, maintainability, observability

## Nice to have (frontend-leaning)

- Context engineering / spec-driven / AI agent workflows
- Responsive SPA development
- Test automation: unit + e2e (Jest, Cypress/Playwright, etc.)
- High-volume consumer-facing platforms
- Domains: Product Creation, Merchandising, Planning, Marketing, Consumer Engagement, Digital Commerce
- Agile (Scrum/Kanban) in global teams

## Offer notes (Zaragoza)

- Hybrid: remote up to ~40%; onsite Zaragoza office ~3 days/week
- Growth, multinational environment, product discounts, etc.

## How the coach should use this

- Grade process for a **senior** bar: clarity, tradeoffs, quality, mentoring tone in review
- Prefer React/TS/state/perf/a11y depth; mention shared foundations only when natural
- Treat AI use as a skill to evaluate, not a cheat
- Do not require Kafka/K8s coding in FE exercises; awareness in debrief is enough
